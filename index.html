Fire TV Clean-Up

Switch off Amazon's ads and tracking, and swap the cluttered home screen for a clean, fast one. Do the steps in order and tick each one when it's done. Nothing here can break your TV, and every change can be undone.

  • Your Fire TV and its remote
  • A Windows PC on the same Wi-Fi as the TV
  • About 30 minutes
0 of 11 steps done

Before you start

Check your TV can do this

  1. On the TV, press ⌂ Home, then go to SettingsMy Fire TVAbout.
    On some TVs, "My Fire TV" is called "Device & Software".
  2. Find the line about the TV's software.
It says "Fire OS" (like Fire OS 7 or 8): great, carry on.
It says "Vega OS": stop here. Amazon locked these newer models, and this kit can't change them.

What gets switched off

  • Amazon's ads and the ad screensaver
  • Tracking of what you watch
  • Shopping, pop-ups and surveys
  • Extras like Amazon Photos, Amazon Music and the Silk browser
  • Amazon's home screen (replaced by Projectivy)

What keeps working

  • Netflix, Hulu, Disney+, YouTube and all your apps
  • Prime Video and the Appstore
  • Alexa voice on the remote
  • Live TV, HDMI inputs and AirPlay

Part 1

Unlock the TV

1
On the TV

Turn on developer mode

  1. Go to SettingsMy Fire TVAbout.
  2. Highlight the very first line (it shows your TV's name).
  3. Press the middle ◉ Select button 7 times, quickly.
  4. A message says "You are now a developer". (If it says you already are one, that's fine too.)
2
On the TV

Turn on ADB debugging

  1. Press ↩ Back once to get to the My Fire TV list.
  2. Open Developer Options. It's new, near the bottom.
  3. Set ADB debugging to ON. If a warning pops up, choose Turn On.

ADB debugging lets your PC send settings to the TV. You'll approve your PC on the TV screen in Step 7, so no other device can use it without you knowing.

3
On the TV

Write down the TV's IP address

  1. Go to SettingsMy Fire TVAboutNetwork.
  2. Find IP Address. It's four numbers with dots, like 192.168.1.57.

Part 2

Get the kit onto your PC

The kit lives on GitHub, a website people use to share code. You don't need an account, you just download it.

4
On the PC

Download the kit

Download the kit (ZIP file)
  1. Click the button above. It saves BespokeTVOS-main.zip to your Downloads folder.

Button not working? Go to github.com/Sebixmc/BespokeTVOS, click the green Code button, then Download ZIP.

5
On the PC

Unblock it, then unzip it

  1. Open your Downloads folder.
  2. Right-click BespokeTVOS-main.zip and choose Properties.
  3. At the bottom of the General tab, tick Unblock, then click OK. (No Unblock box? Skip this.)
  4. Right-click the ZIP again and choose Extract All…, then click Extract.
  5. A new folder opens. Leave it open for the next step.
Why unblock? Windows is suspicious of anything downloaded. Unblocking first means fewer warning pop-ups later.
6
On the PC

Start the kit

  1. In the folder that opened, open BespokeTVOS-main. Sometimes there's a folder inside a folder with the same name, so open that one too.
  2. Double-click START-HERE (it may show as START-HERE.bat, with a gear icon).
  3. If a blue "Windows protected your PC" box appears, click More info, then Run anyway.
  4. If a box asks "Do you want to run this file?", click Run.

A black window opens. The first time, it says "downloading Google's official Android tools". Give it a minute. Then you'll see this menu:

Windows Firewall pop-up? If Windows asks whether to allow "adb", tick Private networks and click Allow access.

Part 3

Connect the PC to the TV

7
PC, then TV

Connect and say yes on the TV

  1. In the black window, type 1 and press Enter.
  2. Type your TV's IP address, from Step 3, and press Enter.
  3. Look at the TV. A box appears asking "Allow USB debugging?"
  4. With the remote, move down to Always allow from this computer and press ◉ Select to tick it.
  5. Move to OK and press ◉ Select.
  6. The PC window says Connected to your TV! in green. Press Enter to go back to the menu.

Part 4

Clean it up

8
On the PC

Run "Do everything"

Type 2 and press Enter. The kit works through four steps and stops between each one so you can read it. Press Enter to move on.

  1. Check and backup. It shows your TV model and saves a list of your apps in a my-tv folder. If it says STOP, your TV runs Vega OS (see "Before you start").
  2. Amazon junk. It asks "Go ahead? (y/n)". Type y and press Enter. A list scrolls by with OK next to each item. A few lines saying "not on this TV" are normal.
  3. New home screen. It downloads and installs Projectivy. This takes about a minute. Your TV switches to Projectivy when it's done. Go to Step 9 now, then come back.
  4. Tracking block. Type y and press Enter. You'll see a few lines saying BLOCKED and a few saying working. That's what you want.
When it says "ALL DONE!" you can close the black window.
9
On the TV

Make Projectivy your home screen

  1. Projectivy is on the TV screen. Click through its welcome screens with ◉ Select.
  2. Open Projectivy's Settings (the gear icon at the top right of its home screen).
  3. Go to General and turn Override current launcher ON.
  4. Press ⌂ Home on the remote. You should land back in Projectivy, not Amazon's screen.
  5. Go back to the PC and press Enter to run the last step.

You might see Amazon's screen flash for a moment when you press Home. That's normal.

Part 5

Check it and make it yours

10
On the TV

Restart and test

  1. Go to SettingsMy Fire TVRestart.
  2. When it's back, press ⌂ Home. You should see Projectivy.
  3. Open two or three of your apps (Netflix, Prime Video, YouTube) and play something in each.

After a restart, the TV quietly switches ADB debugging off. That only matters if you use the kit again. See "It worked before, but now it won't connect" below.

11
On the TV

Arrange your home screen

  • Move, hide or rename an app: highlight it and press ≡ Menu (three lines).
  • Make your own rows (like "Shows" or "Kids"): press ≡ Menu on a row.
  • HDMI inputs (game console, cable box): add input shortcuts so they sit next to your apps.
  • Wallpaper and icons: in Projectivy's Settings. Some extras need Projectivy's paid version.

An AirPlay tile that says "Unable to start app" is normal. That tile only opens AirPlay's settings, and Amazon doesn't let other home screens open it. AirPlay from an iPhone still works, so just hide the tile.

If something goes wrong

Fixes for common problems

"Can't reach the TV"
  1. Make sure the TV is on (not asleep).
  2. Check Developer OptionsADB debugging is ON.
  3. Check the IP address again under AboutNetwork. It can change.
  4. Make sure the PC and TV are on the same Wi-Fi network.
  5. In the black window, type 1 and try again.
It worked before, but now it won't connect

The TV switches ADB debugging off after a restart.

  1. On the TV: Developer OptionsADB debugging. Switch it OFF, then ON again.
  2. On the PC: type 1 and connect again. You won't need to approve it on the TV a second time.
The "Allow USB debugging?" box never appeared
  1. Wake the TV up and stay on any screen.
  2. Switch ADB debugging OFF and ON again.
  3. Type 1 in the black window. The box usually appears within a few seconds.
Double-clicking START-HERE does nothing, or the window closes instantly
  • Make sure you did Extract All in Step 5. Running it from inside the ZIP doesn't work.
  • Make sure you're double-clicking START-HERE inside the extracted folder, not in the ZIP.
The Home button still goes to Amazon's screen
  1. In Projectivy's Settings, check General › Override current launcher is ON.
  2. Still not working? In the black window, type 5 to run the home screen step again.
  3. Restart the TV.
An app stopped working or won't play

The tracking block is the most likely cause, especially for free apps with ads.

  1. Open the kit and type 9 to switch off just the tracking block. (Won't connect? See "It worked before, but now it won't connect" above.)
  2. Try the app again. If it's still broken, type 8 to undo everything.
It says "STOP: this TV is not running Fire OS"

Your TV runs Amazon's newer Vega OS, which is locked down. The kit has changed nothing. You can still turn off some ads yourself: SettingsPreferencesFeatured Content, then switch off video and audio autoplay.

Changed your mind?

Undo everything

  1. Turn on ADB debugging on the TV if it's off (switch it OFF and ON).
  2. Double-click START-HERE again, and type 1 to connect if it isn't already.
  3. Type 8 and press Enter, then y and Enter.
  4. It asks if you also want to remove Projectivy. Type y for Amazon's home screen back, or n to keep Projectivy.
  5. Restart the TV. Everything is back the way Amazon set it up.

Tested on an Insignia 50" F50 Fire TV running Fire OS 8.1.8.
